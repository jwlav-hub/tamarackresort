Lodging profit & loss

Day is actual. Month and Season, in green, are adjusted forecasts, and Season includes lodging pre-opening labor. Total fee income matches the Rooms line on the Summary P&L. Owner rentals are for reference and are not Tamarack revenue. Labor and other expenses here are part of the resort-wide expenses, not in addition to them.

All figures are illustrative sample data, not Tamarack results. The 250 owner-sold units Tamarack manages are a working figure, and lodging types follow the resort's lodging page. The split is assumed: 82 at the Lodge, 91 Village condos, 64 cottages and chalets, and 13 estate homes. Occupancy is planned at about 38% on weekdays, with weekends climbing from 65% at opening to 90% by Christmas and holidays at 90%. Management is planned at 15% of gross room revenue. The other fee terms are assumed: cleaning at $95 to $450 a stay with a three-night average stay, and maintenance at $180 to $620 per unit per month. Tamarack is assumed to pay card and booking fees and its own staff, while owners carry their unit utilities and dues.

Golf profit & loss

Every column is forecast. Season includes spring course preparation. Golf is not in the Summary P&L, because that season is the ski season, Nov 28 to Apr 4.

All figures are illustrative sample data, not Tamarack results. The May 1 to October 15 season, the average round of about $120 and the maximum of 300 rounds a day are planning figures. The rest is assumed: demand climbing from about 37% of the maximum in May to about 75% in midsummer, with Saturdays and holiday weekends busiest; rates that run lower in May and October and higher on weekends; club rentals on about 6% of rounds at $55; two to ten lessons a day at $90; and all course, labor and cart costs.
